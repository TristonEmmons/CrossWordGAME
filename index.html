    How to Play

    QWERT SUNZO ALIME
    1. Words are hidden in the grid — find them all to win!
    2. Click and hold the first letter, drag to the last letter, then let go.
    3. Words can run across, down, and — in later levels — backwards and diagonally.
    4. Stuck? Each level gives you 2 hints. A hint lights up a 5×5 area where a word begins.
    5. Coffee break: tap the coffee cup in the corner of the puzzle to have one word found for you. Finishing new levels brews more coffee, and it brews faster when you use fewer hints.
